Product Brief
What is the product?
A website for people who want to rediscover classic video games from their childhood.

Who is it for?
It’s for people who remember playing a game but may have forgotten its name, as well as people who simply want to find an old game to play again.

What problem does it solve?

People often remember bits and pieces of a game—what the characters looked like, which console they used, or what the gameplay was like—but cannot remember the title. This makes it difficult to find the game through a normal search.

What will the product do?

The website will provide a collection of classic games that people can search and browse. Each game will have useful information and images to help users recognize whether it is the game they remember.

MVP:
Search for classic games
Browse the collection
Open a game and view its details
Find games using information other than just the title
Use the website comfortably on both phones and computers

What is not included:
The website will not sell, download, or launch games. It will focus on helping people find and learn about games they remember.
Features such as user accounts, ratings, reviews, and community contributions can be considered later.
